## Compliance rules (apply to every ad, caption and video)

- Never name prescription-only medicines or brands (weight loss injections, anti-wrinkle products). Promote the **consultation**, not the product.
- No before/after body images, no "lose X stone / X lbs" claims, no timeframes promising results.
- Don't address people's health or body directly ("Are you overweight?", "Struggling with your weight?"). Meta's personal attributes policy rejects this. Use neutral wording: "Thinking about...", "Curious about...".
- Avoid unqualified safety or efficacy claims ("safe", "guaranteed", "proven"). Say "suitable for you, where clinically appropriate".
- Show the clinic's regulator registration (Healthcare Inspectorate Wales) and the nurses' qualifications on the page once confirmed.
- Ask the clinic to approve all clinical statements before publishing. This is their responsibility as the regulated provider.

## Video scripts (vertical 9:16, 20-30 seconds, subtitles on)

Shooting notes for all three: natural light, clinic background, phone held at eye level, talk to camera, no music with lyrics. Film each script in 2-3 takes and use the most natural one. The nurses should say it in their own words; these are guides, not word-for-word.

### Script 1: "What happens at a consultation" (Louise, approx. 25s)

| Time | Visual | Line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Louise to camera, smiling | "Thinking about weight loss support but not sure what happens first?" | **What happens at a consultation?** |
| 3-10s | Louise to camera | "You come in, we sit down and have a proper chat. Your goals, your health, any questions you have." | Step 1: A proper chat |
| 10-17s | Cut to clinic room shot or Andrea nodding | "If treatment is right for you, we explain your options and costs clearly. If it isn't, we'll tell you that too." | Step 2: Honest advice |
| 17-23s | Louise to camera | "There's no pressure to start anything. You decide." | Step 3: Your decision |
| 23-27s | Louise to camera | "Book your free consultation with us in Abersychan." | **Free consultation. Link below.** |

### Script 2: "3 questions to ask any clinic" (Andrea, approx. 28s)

| Time | Visual | Line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Andrea to camera | "Before you book any clinic, ask these three things." | **3 questions to ask any clinic** |
| 3-9s | Andrea, counts on fingers | "One: who will actually be treating me, and what are their qualifications?" | 1. Who treats me? |
| 9-15s | Andrea | "Two: will I have a proper consultation first, or will I be rushed into something?" | 2. Is there a consultation? |
| 15-21s | Andrea | "Three: is the pricing clear before I commit?" | 3. Is pricing clear? |
| 21-28s | Andrea, smiling | "At Eden, you'll always see a nurse prescriber, and we'll always explain everything first. Book a free consultation." | **Eden Aesthetics, Abersychan** |
