How we'd advertise (and what we won't do)

Ads promote the consultation, not specific prescription-only treatments. UK rules (ASA/MHRA) and Meta's policies restrict advertising these products to the public, and we won't put your clinic at risk. You'll approve every ad, caption and page statement before it goes live.
